Free Community Health Camp 2025

Published on November 6, 2025 | By MediCare CSR Team

MediCare Plus is pleased to announce our annual Free Community Health Camp offering check-ups, blood tests, and medical consultations for all age groups.

🏥 Services Offered

  • General health check-ups
  • Free blood pressure and sugar testing
  • Specialist consultations for cardiology and pediatrics

This initiative aims to raise community awareness and make healthcare accessible to everyone, especially in underserved areas.
