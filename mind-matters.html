Mind Matters by Dr. Kavindya Fernando

Published on November 6, 2025 | By Dr. Kavindya Fernando

Dr. Kavindya explores strategies for managing stress, anxiety, and maintaining a positive mental outlook in daily life.

🧘 Importance of Mental Health

Mental health affects how we think, feel, and act. Prioritizing it leads to better relationships and productivity.

💡 Simple Wellness Practices

  • Practice mindfulness or meditation daily.
  • Maintain healthy social connections.
  • Get adequate rest and avoid burnout.
