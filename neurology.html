About Our Neurology Services

The Neurology Department at MediCare Plus offers comprehensive evaluation and treatment of neurological conditions including stroke, epilepsy, migraines, Parkinson’s disease, and neuropathies. Our advanced diagnostic facilities and expert neurologists ensure personalized care for every patient.

Our Neurology Treatments

EEG (Electroencephalogram)

Measures brain activity to diagnose seizures, epilepsy, and brain disorders.

MRI Brain Scan

Advanced imaging technique to detect strokes, tumors, and neurological injuries.

Stroke Management

Emergency and rehabilitative care for patients with ischemic and hemorrhagic strokes.

Neurosurgery

State-of-the-art surgical procedures for complex brain and spinal conditions.
