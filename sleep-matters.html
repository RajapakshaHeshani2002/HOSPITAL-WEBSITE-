Sleep Matters: Recharge Your Mind and Body

Published on November 6, 2025 | By UnityCare Sleep Center

Quality sleep is vital for a healthy mind and body. Adults need around 7–8 hours of sleep each night to perform at their best and maintain emotional balance.

🛏 Why Sleep is Important

  • Improves memory and learning.
  • Boosts the immune system.
  • Reduces stress and irritability.
  • Enhances focus and productivity.

🌜 Tips for Better Sleep

Maintain a regular sleep schedule, avoid screens before bedtime, and create a relaxing environment in your bedroom. Limit caffeine intake after noon.
