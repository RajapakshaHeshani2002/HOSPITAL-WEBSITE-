About Our Cardiology Services

At MediCare Plus, our Cardiology Department specializes in diagnosing, treating, and preventing heart-related diseases. We provide comprehensive care for conditions like hypertension, coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, and heart failure.

Our Cardiology Treatments

Angioplasty & Stent Placement

Minimally invasive procedure to open blocked arteries and restore blood flow.

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

Non-invasive test that records the electrical activity of your heart.

Echocardiogram

Ultrasound-based test to visualize heart structure and function in real-time.

Heart Bypass Surgery

Advanced surgical procedures to treat severe coronary artery blockages.
