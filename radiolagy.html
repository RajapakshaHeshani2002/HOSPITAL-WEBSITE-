About Our Radiology Services

The Radiology Department at MediCare Plus provides state-of-the-art imaging services including X-rays, CT scans, MRI, and Ultrasound. Our experienced radiologists ensure accurate and timely diagnosis for better patient care.

Our Radiology Services

X-Ray Imaging

Quick and precise imaging to identify bone fractures and chest issues.

CT Scan

Detailed cross-sectional imaging for accurate diagnosis of internal organs.

MRI Scan

Non-invasive imaging for detailed visualization of soft tissues and organs.

Ultrasound

Safe and effective imaging for pregnancy, abdomen, and soft tissue examination.
