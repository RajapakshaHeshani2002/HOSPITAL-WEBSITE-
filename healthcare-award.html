MediCare Wins Excellence in Healthcare Award

Published on November 6, 2025 | By UnityCare PR Department

MediCare Plus has been honored with the National Excellence in Healthcare Award 2025 for outstanding service quality, innovation, and patient satisfaction.

🏅 About the Award

The award recognizes hospitals that maintain high standards in patient care, safety, and clinical innovation across Sri Lanka.

We thank our dedicated medical teams and support staff for their commitment and compassion that made this achievement possible.
