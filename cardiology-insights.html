Cardiology Insights by Dr. Amal Perera

Published on November 6, 2025 | By Dr. Amal Perera

Cardiologist Dr. Amal Perera discusses the importance of early diagnosis and lifestyle management in preventing heart disease.

❤️ Common Risk Factors

  • High blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Smoking and poor diet
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Chronic stress

🫀 Prevention Tips

Maintain a balanced diet, exercise regularly, and attend annual heart check-ups to monitor your cardiovascular health.
